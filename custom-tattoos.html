Custom Tattoo FAQ

How much does a custom tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, complexity, style, and artist. Consultations are free. Get an accurate quote from your chosen artist.

How long does custom design take?

Design time varies. Simple designs may take days; complex work may take weeks. We'll give you a timeline during consultation.

Can I bring my own design or reference?

Absolutely. Reference images, sketches, and ideas are perfect. Our artists will adapt your concept to work on skin.

What if I don't like the design?

We'll revise it. Your satisfaction is our priority. We keep refining until the design is exactly right.

Do you do deposits?

Yes. A deposit holds your appointment date. Deposits are non-refundable if you cancel with less than 48 hours' notice.

Are touch-ups included?

Yes. Touch-ups on your custom tattoo by the same artist are complimentary within a reasonable timeframe after your session.
