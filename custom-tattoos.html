Custom work

Your idea, built for your skin.

Bring us a clear direction, a reference, or a conversation. The right artist can help turn it into a tattoo that feels like yours.

Start with the artist.

Every artist has a different voice. Look through the portfolios on the home page, choose the work that feels closest to your idea, and contact that artist directly.

What to send

  • A short description of what you want.
  • Reference images or examples of what you like.
  • Placement and approximate size.
  • Color or black-and-grey preference.
  • Your preferred timing and best way to reach you.

What happens next

Custom work is discussed with the artist before the appointment. We do not quote tattoos online or over the phone because size, placement, detail, and artist all matter. A deposit may be required to hold an appointment.

Want to talk it through? Call (661) 395-0639 or visit 1518 18th Street in Downtown Bakersfield.

Good to know

Take your time choosing the right piece.

A strong tattoo starts with a good plan. We would rather answer questions up front than rush you into a design that is not right.

Can I bring an existing drawing?

Yes. Bring your own artwork or references and discuss how it can be adapted.

Can I book a large project?

Yes. Larger work usually starts with a consultation and may take multiple sessions.

How do I get a price?

Contact the artist or visit the shop. Tattoos are quoted in person.

Do you take walk-ins for custom work?

Small custom ideas may fit as a walk-in when an artist is available. Call first.
