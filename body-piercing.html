Body Piercing Questions Answered

Does body piercing hurt?

Most people describe it as quick and sharp rather than painful. The needle is in and out fast. Pain tolerance varies by individual and body location.

How long do piercings take to heal?

Ear piercings typically heal in 4–6 weeks. Other body piercings take longer (3–6 months). Our team will give you specific healing timelines.

Can I change my jewelry right away?

No. Leave initial jewelry in place during the entire healing period. Changing it too early risks infection or incomplete healing.

What if I want one piercing or multiple?

Single or multiple piercings — we do them all. Multiple piercings can be done in one session if desired.

Body Piercing FAQ

Can you pierce if I'm pregnant?

We recommend consulting your doctor first. Some piercings are fine during pregnancy; others are not. We'll discuss your situation.

What should I do if my piercing gets infected?

Contact us immediately. Mild infections can be treated with proper aftercare. Serious infections require medical attention.

Can I get pierced if I'm under 18?

Most piercings require legal guardian consent if you're under 18. Some states have specific age requirements. Call us to ask.

Do you pierce with guns or needles?

We use sterile needles. Guns are associated with more complications and tissue damage. Needles are the professional standard.

Can I remove my piercing later?

Yes. If you decide a piercing isn't for you, it can be removed anytime. Some piercings close; others may leave minimal scarring.

What jewelry materials do you use?

Titanium, surgical steel, solid gold (implant-grade), and niobium. We avoid cheap metals that cause reactions.
