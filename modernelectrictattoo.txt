FAQ PAGE
How much does a tattoo cost?
Tattoo pricing varies by size, complexity, placement, and artist. Our minimum starts at [confirm with shop — usually $80–$100 for small pieces]. Custom work is quoted per project. Flash tattoos from Matt Ryan start at $100–$150. The best way to get an accurate quote is to reach out through Instagram DM with your idea and reference images.
Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
Both! Appointments are recommended for larger, more complex custom work. Walk-ins are welcome based on artist availability. Call ahead at (661) 395-0639 to check same-day availability.
How do I prepare for my tattoo?
Eat a full meal 1–2 hours before your appointment. Stay hydrated. Get a good night's sleep. Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours before your session. Moisturize the area for a week before (but don't apply lotion the day of). Wear clothing that gives easy access to the area being tattooed.
Does it hurt?
Yes — but the level of discomfort varies hugely by placement, your pain tolerance, and how long the session runs. Ribs, sternum, hands, and feet are typically more sensitive. Arms, thighs, and upper back are usually more manageable. Our artists will always check in with you throughout the session.
How do I care for my new tattoo?
Your artist will give you specific aftercare instructions. General guidance: keep it clean, keep it moisturized, keep it out of direct sunlight and water for the first 2–3 weeks. Don't pick or scratch the peeling skin. Follow up with your artist if anything looks unusual.
Do you do cover-ups?
Yes. Cover-ups require an in-person consultation so the artist can assess the existing tattoo and determine what's achievable. Not every tattoo can be covered — sometimes laser lightening sessions may be recommended first. Reach out to set up a consultation.
Do you offer piercings?
Yes! We offer body piercing services. Contact us for current availability and pricing.
Do you sell gift certificates?
Absolutely. Gift certificates are available in-shop at any denomination. Stop by 1518 18th Street during business hours.
