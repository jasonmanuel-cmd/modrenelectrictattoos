Cover-ups & reworks

Make room for something better.

A cover-up starts with an honest look at the old tattoo and a plan for the new one.

Every cover-up is different.

Existing ink, age, color, placement, and skin all affect what is possible. Our artists can help you understand the strongest direction for the area instead of promising a one-size-fits-all result.

Bring this to the consultation

  • A clear photo of the existing tattoo, if you are contacting an artist first.
  • Ideas for the new subject, style, and color.
  • Your preferred size and placement.
  • Any limits you want the artist to know about.

What to expect

Cover-ups are quoted after an artist sees the existing work. Some pieces can be handled directly; others may need a larger design or a staged plan. The artist will explain the options before you book.

There is no judgment here. Bring the tattoo you want to change and let’s talk about what can work.

Cover-up questions

Plan the new tattoo, not just the old one.

Can every tattoo be covered?

Not every tattoo can be covered with every design. An artist needs to assess it in person.

Can I choose any design?

The best design is one that can create enough coverage while still looking intentional.

Will a cover-up cost more?

Pricing depends on size, detail, and time. The artist will quote it after the consultation.

Can you rework someone else’s tattoo?

Yes. Bring it in and let the artist see what needs to change.
