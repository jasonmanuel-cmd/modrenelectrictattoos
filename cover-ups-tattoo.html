Cover-Up Tattoo Questions

Can you cover any tattoo?

Most tattoos can be covered or significantly improved. Tattoos that are very dark, very large, or in awkward spots are more challenging. We'll be honest about what's possible.

Will it look natural?

Yes, if designed right. A good cover-up uses the old tattoo as part of the design or completely obscures it with bold new work. Placement and design matter.

Do cover-ups cost more?

They often do, because they require more skill and thought. Exact pricing depends on the old tattoo and new design. Get a quote during consultation.

How long until I can see healed results?

Final results appear after 2–3 weeks of healing. Tattoos continue to settle for up to three months.

Cover-Up Tattoo FAQ

Will a cover-up be darker than the original?

Often yes. Darker ink covers lighter ink better. But our artists design clever cover-ups that work with color and placement, not just darkness.

Can I choose any design for my cover-up?

We work with you to design something that achieves coverage. Some designs work better than others depending on the old tattoo's size, color, and location.

What's your cover-up success rate?

Very high. Our artists have years of experience. We discuss realistic expectations upfront so there are no surprises.

How soon after getting covered can I get a touch-up?

Wait 2–3 weeks for initial healing. After full healing (2–3 months), we can assess whether touch-ups are needed.

Do you keep before & after photos?

Yes. We love documenting cover-up transformations. We'll ask for permission to use yours (with or without identifying info).

Can I cover up a tattoo from another artist?

Absolutely. Doesn't matter who did the original — our artists will transform it into something new.
