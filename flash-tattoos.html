Flash Tattoo FAQ

How long does a flash tattoo take?

Most flash tattoos take 15 minutes to an hour, depending on size and complexity. Plan accordingly.

What about pain?

Tattoo pain varies by body placement and individual. Most flash tattoos are small enough to tolerate easily for first-timers.

Do I need an ID?

Yes. You must be 18+ with a valid government photo ID. Bring it when you arrive.

What should I do to prepare?

Eat a full meal, wear comfortable clothing that exposes your tattoo area, and get a good night's sleep beforehand.

How much are typical flash tattoos?

$100–$150 depending on size and design. Ask your artist for exact pricing when you arrive.

What if I don't like anything on the wall?

You can request custom work, but that's not flash pricing. Or check back — new flash is added regularly.
