Flash & walk-ins

Walk in when the timing is right.

Flash is a simple way to get tattooed today. Choose from available designs or ask what an artist can fit into the schedule.

Classic designs. Same-day energy.

Flash tattoos are pre-drawn designs made to be tattooed as shown or with small adjustments. They are a good choice when you want something straightforward and do not want to wait for a full custom design process.

Before you come in

  • Call (661) 395-0639 to ask who is available.
  • Bring valid ID and know where you want the tattoo.
  • Have a few backup ideas in case the first design is not available.
  • Eat beforehand and wear clothing that gives access to the area.

Availability and pricing

Walk-in space, flash designs, and pricing change by artist and day. Call ahead for current information rather than relying on an old post or price list.

Walk-ins are welcome when artists have time. First come does not always mean immediate service, so calling first is the best way to avoid a wasted trip.
