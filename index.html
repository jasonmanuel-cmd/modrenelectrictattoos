About Modern Electric

A serious shop with a welcoming front door.

Modern Electric Tattoo Company opened in Downtown Bakersfield in 2011. Our goal is simple: give people a clean, professional place to get tattooed by artists with their own styles and standards.

Whether you are getting your first small piece, building a larger project, or looking for a cover-up, start with the artist whose work speaks to you.

FAQ

Good questions before you come in.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, when an artist has time. Call ahead for current availability and wait times.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on the artist, size, placement, and detail. We quote in person, not online or over the phone.

What should I bring?

Bring valid ID, a reference or clear idea, and comfortable clothing that gives access to the tattoo area.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended for larger or custom work. Walk-ins depend on the day and artist availability.

Do cover-ups require a consultation?

Yes. The artist needs to see the existing tattoo before explaining what is possible.

Can I bring a friend?

Ask your artist before bringing a guest. Procedure space is limited and comfort comes first.
